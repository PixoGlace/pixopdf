Privacy by design

Your files never leave your device.

PixoPDF performs PDF operations on your computer. Your documents, filenames and contents are never sent to a remote service.

No PDF, file path or document metadata is included in an update request.

Local processing

Importing, thumbnail generation, editing and exporting all happen directly on your machine.

No upload, no account

Work offline without creating a profile or trusting your files to a cloud provider.

Limited and transparent connectivity

Optional update checks contact only GitHub’s public Releases API.
